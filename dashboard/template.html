Country extremes: highest and lowest points

The 249 officially assigned ISO 3166-1 codes, with highest and lowest points from the Wikipedia table, List of elevation extremes by country. Åland and Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba are not in that table: their highest points come from their own Wikipedia articles and their lowest points are set to sea level. No elevation model is used. Countries made of several map pieces are drawn as one shape. Places in the table without an ISO code (Clipperton Island, Bir Tawil, Kosovo, Spratly Islands, Ashmore and Cartier Islands, Coral Sea Islands) are left out. Climate extremes are not yet included.

Status tags: clean = no issue found; unnamed = no named point; disputed = disputed territory or competing candidates; span mismatch = Wikipedia's span does not equal highest minus lowest; shared = lowest point shared with a neighbour or several coasts. Every figure is the one in the Wikipedia table; countries the table does not list are not included. Marker positions come from Wikidata and are shown only where the named point matched unambiguously.
